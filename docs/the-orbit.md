# THE ORBIT — experience plan

Third direction, and the first one built on a diagnosis rather than a guess. Written
before code, per brief.

---

## Why the last two failed

Worth recording so it does not happen a third time.

- **Floating slabs** — ambient wallpaper. Looped forever, said nothing, ignored scroll.
- **The Automation Core** — technically sound, conceptually illegible. Three dark plates
  stacked on a dark ground read as *an object*, not as *your business*. The failure was
  **silhouette**: a rectangular stack has no recognisable parts, so there was nothing for
  a first-time visitor to decode.

**The lesson THE ORBIT is built on:** a central mass with three visibly distinct
satellites is readable in one glance, before any copy is read. One brand, three
capabilities, connected. The structure carries the meaning even at a thumbnail.

## 3. Visual concept

**One Brand World, three capabilities in orbit, connected by signal.**

Every service does the same thing at different scale: takes noise in, returns signal.
The ecosystem makes that literal — each orbiting object performs its own noise→signal
transformation, then fires one clean pulse to the centre. Stage 7 is all three arriving
at once. That is the whole pitch, told in geometry.

---

## 4. The Brand World

Not a planet. A **precision-engineered sphere**, roughly 2.4 units radius.

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Shell | Dark matte composite, faceted into subtle geometric panels, fine engraved seams |
| Seams | Recessed channels that illuminate progressively as each service connects |
| Aperture | One equatorial band of smoked translucent glass showing controlled internal light |
| Edges | Brushed dark metal chamfers on panel boundaries, catching the key light only |
| Core | A single internal light source — dim at Stage 1, unified warm gold by Stage 8 |

**Movement:** never free rotation. Slight turns at Stages 1, 2 and 8; completely still at
Stages 3 and 7 so copy can be read.

## 5–6. The three orbiting objects

Shared language: dark composite bodies, machined metal edges, one accent light each,
comparable mass. Different silhouettes so they are never confused.

### 01 — Maps Lead Scraper · `#35e0c4`

**Form:** a faceted octahedral prism, roughly 0.55 units, with a recessed lattice face.

**Behaviour:** scattered markers drift in the volume around it → drawn inward → duplicates
extinguish on contact → survivors snap into six ordered rows on the lattice face → one
teal stream departs for the Brand World.

*Means:* Maps chaos becomes a deduplicated, scored, contactable list.

### 02 — OrderRise · `#ffa63d`

**Form:** a layered torus with three concentric routed channels, roughly 0.7 units across.

**Behaviour:** fragmented pulses enter the outer channel at irregular intervals → split
across three routes → two routes dim and close → surviving pulses merge in the inner
channel → one continuous amber signal exits → confirmation pulse to the centre.

*Means:* messy customer messages become one structured order.

### 03 — Custom AI Agents · `#8f7cff`

**Form:** seven modular components — no enclosing body — that assemble into a coherent
polyhedral frame.

**Behaviour:** components hang disconnected and unlit → rise and rotate into alignment →
matching faces meet and connection struts form → nodes light in sequence → one pulse
travels the completed frame → violet signal to the centre.

*Means:* tools, APIs and business rules become one tailored system.

## 7–8. Eight-stage storyboard

| Stage | Scroll | Question answered | Scene |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Enter the atmosphere | 0–10% | What is this? | Extreme macro on the shell. One lit seam. Sphere unrecognisable as a sphere |
| 2 · Reveal the world | 10–22% | Who is this? | Pull back to three-quarter. Full silhouette. Seams glow faintly. **Stillness** |
| 3 · Activate the orbit | 22–34% | What is offered? | Sphere still. Three paths ignite in sequence. Objects enter and settle. **Stillness** |
| 4 · Service one | 34–48% | How does 01 help? | Camera travels the teal orbit. Markers converge, dedupe, order. **Stillness** |
| 5 · Service two | 48–62% | How does 02 help? | Camera arcs behind the sphere to amber. Signals route, merge, exit. **Stillness** |
| 6 · Service three | 62–76% | How does 03 help? | Lower approach to violet. Components assemble and ignite. **Stillness** |
| 7 · Align the ecosystem | 76–90% | Why the whole system? | Wide. Three signals arrive together. Core unifies. **Deep stillness for proof** |
| 8 · Final destination | 90–100% | What now? | Balanced final composition. Accents resolve to one gold light. CTA |

Four scheduled stillness points. The scene stops so copy can be read — that discipline is
what separates this from a showreel.

## 11. Lighting states

Base rig: soft key upper-left, restrained cool fill right, rim behind. Plus one point
light per service at its own accent colour.

- Stages 1–2: key 1.4, all service lights at 0.
- Stage 3: each service light rises to 0.35 as its path ignites.
- Stages 4–6: active service to 2.2, the other two drop to 0.25 — **dimmed, never black**.
- Stage 7: all three equalise at 1.1; the sphere core rises to `--color-unified`.
- Stage 8: services settle to 0.8, core at full gold, background lifts ~6%.

## 17–19. Architecture

```
webgl/orbit/
  stage.tsx           Canvas, quality tier, visibility + resize
  brand-world.tsx     Central sphere, panels, seams, core light
  objects/
    lead-prism.tsx    01 — converge, dedupe, order
    comms-torus.tsx   02 — route, discard, merge
    agent-frame.tsx   03 — assemble, connect, ignite
  orbits.tsx          Three paths + travelling points
  signals.tsx         Instanced particles for all transfers
  camera-rig.tsx      Sole writer of camera transform
  lighting-rig.tsx    Key/fill/rim + three service lights
  timeline.ts         Master GSAP timeline, 8 labels
  quality.ts          Capability probe → high | balanced | lightweight
```

**Scene graph:** `Scene → [LightingRig, BrandWorld → [shell, seams, core], Orbits → [path×3], Objects → [prism, torus, frame], Signals]`

**One GSAP timeline** with the eight labels from the brief, scrubbed by one ScrollTrigger.
Nothing else may write camera, sphere rotation, object position or lighting — competing
ScrollTriggers are the single most common way these builds break.

## 20. Assets

Geometry generated in code — no GLTF pipeline, near-zero payload. Fonts already loaded.
**Still needed from you:** product screenshots, a portrait, three real proof numbers.

## 21–23. Performance, accessibility, fallback

Three tiers from a real capability probe (device memory, cores, GPU string, `powerPreference` result) — never screen width alone. Instanced particles, shared materials, DPR capped 1.75/1.25, render paused on tab blur, full disposal on unmount.

Canvas is `aria-hidden`; all copy is semantic HTML, statically prerendered. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, correct heading order, no hover-only information, no scroll locking. Lightweight tier drops the canvas entirely for a static composition — every word and both CTAs still present.
